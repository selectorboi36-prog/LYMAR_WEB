Official school information

School

Lycée Saint Marcel de Rukara

A Catholic secondary school in Rukara, Kayonza District, Eastern Province of Rwanda.

Location

Rukara Sector, Rukara Parish, Kayonza District, Eastern Province.

The school is approximately 7 km from the Kayonza–Nyagatare main road.

History

The school began as École Secondaire de Rukara (ESR), through an initiative of parents in Rukara and priests of the Catholic Diocese of Kibungo.

It began with 75 students: 50 students in S1 and 25 students in S2.

Recognition

The school began on 26 September 2000 and was officially recognized by the government from the 2000/2001 school year.

School Partnership

Lycée Saint Marcel de Rukara is associated with the Catholic Diocese of Kibungo.

According to the school's official website, from 2023 it has worked with the government under an agreement.

Current Academic Streams

The school's official website lists:

  • Mathematics and Science Stream 2
  • Languages
  • Arts and Humanities

Language Section

In 2010, the school was approved to open the LKK language section:

English – Kiswahili – Kinyarwanda

MCE Section

In 2014, the school was approved to start MCE:

Mathematics – Computer – Economics

Support & Development

The school website describes support from sisters of the Sacred Heart of Jesus and Mary, together with funding from the Spanish organization Manos Unidas.
